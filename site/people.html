The founder

Prof. Dr. Abdallah Attia

A visionary in shaping the urban fabric of the region. He founded PUD on the synergy between people and their natural and cultural environments, planned much of Egypt's North West Coast and taught a generation of planners and architects.

Prof. Dr. Abdallah Attia trained in Alexandria and Zurich and founded PUD in Cairo in 1966 on one idea: planning and architecture should answer the place, not repeat a formula. He planned much of Egypt's North West Coast, led Marina El Alamein from master plan to site supervision, and designed Tut Amun on Lake Nasser and Hilton Dahab on the Gulf of Aqaba. Marina El Alamein won the State Encouragement Prize in Architecture in 1990. He taught as Professor of Architecture and chaired the Urban Planning Department at Ain Shams University.

  • 1956Graduates in architecture from Alexandria University and joins its faculty.
  • 1962Doctorat d'État in architecture and planning, ETH Zurich.
  • 1966Founds PUD in Cairo.
  • 1975Professor of Architecture.
  • 1987Chairs the Urban Planning Department at Ain Shams University until 1992.
  • 1990State Encouragement Prize in Architecture for Marina El Alamein.
