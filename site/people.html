The founder

Prof. Dr. Abdallah Attia

Prof. Dr. Abdallah Abdel Aziz Attia founded PUD in Cairo in 1966. He trained as an architect in Alexandria and took his doctorate at ETH Zurich.

His best-known work sits in places with strong natural conditions: Marina El Alamein on the Mediterranean, Tut Amun on Lake Nasser, Hilton Dahab under the Sinai mountains. Marina El Alamein won the State Encouragement Prize in Architecture in 1990. He prepared tourism and structure plans for Egypt's North West Coast, and as Professor of Architecture and head of the Urban Planning Department at Ain Shams University he taught a generation of planners and architects.

  • 1956Graduates in architecture from Alexandria University and joins its faculty.
  • 1962Doctorat d'État in architecture and planning, ETH Zurich.
  • 1966Founds PUD in Cairo.
  • 1975Professor of Architecture.
  • 1987Chairs the Urban Planning Department at Ain Shams University until 1992.
  • 1990State Encouragement Prize in Architecture for Marina El Alamein.
