Sumou Boulevard, New Cairo

Mixed-use development · Mostakbal City, New Cairo, Egypt

Mixed useAdvisory
LocationNew Cairo, Egypt
Year2026
ClientParagon-Adeer
RoleSteering committee advisory: supplied the subject-matter experts who review the design work

Sumou Boulevard is a mixed-use development at the gate of Mostakbal City in New Cairo, for Paragon-Adeer. It is built around a 2 km pedestrian boulevard with workspaces and leisure for young professionals. PUD supplied the subject-matter-expert group advising the steering committee that reviews the design, and supported consultant selection.
