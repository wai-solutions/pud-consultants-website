Tut Amun Village Hotel and Resort

Hospitality project · Aswan, Egypt

HospitalityTourism
LocationAswan, Egypt
Year1981
ClientMisr Aswan for Tourism Development

Tut Amun is a village hotel and resort on Lake Nasser in Aswan, designed in 1981 and completed in 1984. Its architecture follows the local Nubian tradition, in a search for a form that is both authentic and contemporary. It later received a certificate as best architectural and urban tourism project (mid-1990s).
