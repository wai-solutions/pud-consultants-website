New Administrative Capital: Strategic Advisory, Phases 2 to 4

Master plan · New Administrative Capital, Egypt

MasterplanAdvisory
LocationNew Administrative Capital, Egypt
Year2025
ClientACUD (Administrative Capital for Urban Development)
RoleStrategic advisory and master planning support; consultant selection support and steering committee coordination

Egypt's New Administrative Capital is planned for 6.5 million residents across 220,000 feddan. PUD says it led the international Steering Committee advising ACUD on the strategic master plan for Phases 2 to 4, the 180,000 feddan beyond Phase 1, with Dar Al-Handasah as lead designer. The plan ties urban growth to ecological systems and revises the city's economic and development strategy.
