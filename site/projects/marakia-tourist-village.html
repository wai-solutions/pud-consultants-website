Marakia Tourist Village

Tourism development · North West Coast (km 50-52 west of Alexandria), Egypt

TourismMasterplanLandscape
LocationNorth West Coast (km 50-52 west of Alexandria), Egypt
Year1981-1983
ClientMinistry of Reconstruction, Government of Egypt
RoleInitial concept and master planning (1981-1983); tourism policy integration; landscape and environmental strategy

Pilot project for coastal development on the North West Coast between km 50 and 52 west of Alexandria, conceived as a tourism destination for about 10,000 visitors. PUD developed the initial design concept; implementation later passed to Noaman & Associates, who re-planned the site.
