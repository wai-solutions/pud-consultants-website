Pyramids Plateau Destination Master Plan

Master plan · Giza, Egypt

MasterplanTourismHeritage
LocationGiza, Egypt
Year2024-2025
ClientNUCA, TDA and Giza Governorate
RoleCultural-heritage specialist on WATG's team: masterplanning and urban advisory, stakeholder management, archaeology and Egyptology advisory, heritage and cultural landscape conservation

Tourism master plan for the Giza Pyramids plateau, from Sphinx International Airport to the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Memphis and its Necropolis. WATG leads; PUD is cultural-heritage specialist on the team. The final framework was presented to the Ministry of Tourism in December 2025.
