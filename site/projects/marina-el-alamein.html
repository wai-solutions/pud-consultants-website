Marina El Alamein

Master plan · North Coast, Egypt

MasterplanTourism
LocationNorth Coast, Egypt
Year1985-1997
ClientNUCA, Ministry of Housing
RoleMaster planning, detailed design and site supervision

Master planning, detailed design and site supervision for the 2,000 hectare Marina El Alamein tourist centre on Egypt's North Coast, for NUCA, Ministry of Housing. Built in phases from 1985, it won the State Encouragement Prize in Architecture in 1990 and became the subject of Prof. Dr. Amr Attia's 1994 London master's thesis.
