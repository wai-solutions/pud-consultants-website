AlUla Strategic Framework Plan

Regional plan · AlUla, Saudi Arabia

Regional planningMasterplan
LocationAlUla, Saudi Arabia
Year2022
ClientRoyal Commission for AlUla (RCU)
RoleLocal partner to WATG

County-wide strategic framework plan for the Royal Commission for AlUla, led by WATG with PUD as local partner. Eisenhower Fellowships' profile of Prof. Dr. Amr Attia says the plans now work as binding planning instruments within a development programme above $15 billion.

AlUla: AlUla is an ancient Arabian oasis city and governorate in the Madinah Region of Saudi Arabia, 350 kilometres northwest of the city of Madinah. Source
