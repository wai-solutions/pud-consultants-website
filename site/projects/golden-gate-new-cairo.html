Golden Gate, New Cairo

Mixed-use development · New Cairo, Egypt

Mixed useMasterplan
LocationNew Cairo, Egypt
Year2020-2021
ClientRedcon Properties
RoleMaster planning, with WATG

Golden Gate is a mixed-use business and retail district in New Cairo for Redcon Properties: 29 commercial and administrative buildings on about 160,000 m², with some 250,000 m² of built-up area. Shops, cafés and restaurants line its pedestrian streets and plazas at ground level, under offices with green roofs, and its main frontage faces South 90 Street. PUD worked on the master plan with WATG.
