Hilton Dahab Village Hotel

Hospitality project · Dahab, Sinai, Egypt

HospitalityTourism
LocationDahab, Egypt
Year1991
ClientMisr Hotels Company
RoleUrban planning, architecture and landscape design

Hilton Dahab is a village hotel on the Gulf of Aqaba in Sinai, designed in 1991 for Misr Hotels Company and built in the mid-1990s. Its rooms sit around linked lagoons below the mountains, in forms drawn from Bedouin building.
