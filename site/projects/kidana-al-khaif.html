Kidana Al Khaif (Mina, Muzdalifah and Arafat)

Master plan · Makkah (Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafat), Saudi Arabia

MasterplanAdvisory
LocationMakkah, Saudi Arabia
Year2024-2026
ClientKidana Development Company (KDC), on behalf of the Royal Commission for Makkah City and Holy Sites (RCMC)
RoleDesign advisory and design review: brief validation, strategic master planning and urban oversight, subject-matter experts panel, landscape and environmental planning, crowd and pilgrimage management, infrastructure and investment strategy

Advisory and master planning for the holy sites of Mina (7.8 km2), Muzdalifah (11.9 km2) and Arafat (12.2 km2) in Makkah, for Kidana Development Company on behalf of the Royal Commission for Makkah City and Holy Sites. The work is ongoing in 2026.
