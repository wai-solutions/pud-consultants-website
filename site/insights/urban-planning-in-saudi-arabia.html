Urban planning in Saudi Arabia: giga-projects, holy sites and regional plans

Saudi Arabia is planning at every scale at once: national land strategies, giga-project destinations, holy-site programmes and the growth of Riyadh, Jeddah and Makkah.

Destinations and heritage

AlUla, Khaybar, Sharaan, the Red Sea coast and the Asir mountains are being planned as heritage and nature destinations. PUD works on AlUla and Khaybar for the Royal Commission for AlUla and on several competition and pre-concept plans with WATG.

Holy sites and cities

The holy sites of Makkah and the growth of Jeddah, Madinah, Taif and Jazan are a long-running planning programme. PUD advised on the Al Mashaaer master plan and Jabal Thabeer park for Kidana, and worked with AECOM Arabia on Jeddah, Taif and Jazan plans.

Regional plans and housing

Sub-regional plans for the Red Sea coast, land strategies for the Saudi Railway Organization and a density and housing framework for Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam show the shift from single sites to systems. PUD Saudi opened in Riyadh in 2018.

Related question

Does PUD have an office in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. PUD Saudi was co-founded in Riyadh in 2018 by Prof. Dr. Amr Attia. The Riyadh office is in the Diplomatic Quarter.
