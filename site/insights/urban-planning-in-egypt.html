Insights

Urban planning in Egypt: from the Nile Valley to the new coast and capital

Most of Egypt's people live on a small share of its land, along the Nile and Delta, so national planning has long pushed growth towards new communities, coasts and desert corridors.

New communities

Egypt has built successive generations of new communities and cities around Cairo and on the desert edges. PUD has planned several of them, including 6th of October City and Sheikh Zayed City developments, New Cairo projects such as Heliopark, and strategic advisory on the New Administrative Capital.

The coasts

The North West Coast and the Red Sea coast became tourism and residential regions from the late 1970s. PUD prepared the North West Coast plans, Marina El Alamein, Marina Matrouh and the Alamein Riviera vision, as well as projects at Ain El Sokhna, Hurghada, Dahab and Marsa Alam.

Cities and heritage

Renewal of historic districts, university campuses and city strategies for places such as Tanta, Mansoura and Alexandria sit beside the new-city programme. PUD is the cultural-heritage specialist on the WATG team for the Giza Pyramids Plateau destination plan.

Related question

Who planned Marina El Alamein?

Prof. Dr. Abdallah Attia and PUD prepared the master plan, detailed design and site supervision for Marina El Alamein for the New Urban Communities Authority (NUCA) from the 1980s.
