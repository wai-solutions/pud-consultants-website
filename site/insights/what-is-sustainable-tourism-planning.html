Insights

What is sustainable tourism planning?

Sustainable tourism planning keeps visitor numbers, buildings and infrastructure in balance with local communities, culture and the environment, so a destination stays worth visiting.

The idea

A destination is sustainable when it can keep its visitors, its residents and its landscape in good condition for the long term. Planning decides how many and which kinds of visitors a place can host, where hotels and services go, and what is protected.

The tools

Tourism master plans, carrying-capacity studies, heritage and environmental protection zones, transport and access plans, and standards for hotels and operators are the usual tools. Programmes such as the Green Star Hotels initiative in Egypt, which PUD worked on with GIZ, set practical standards for operators.

PUD's experience

Tourism planning is PUD's oldest specialism. It runs from the North West Coast and Marina El Alamein plans of the late 1970s and 1980s to destination plans today at the Pyramids Plateau, Port Ghalib, AlUla and Makkah.

Related question

Why is tourism planning important?

Without planning, tourism can overload infrastructure, damage ecosystems and heritage and push residents out. Planning spreads benefits and protects the assets visitors come for.
