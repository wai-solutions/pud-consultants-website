Insights

What is master planning?

A master plan shows how one defined area, a district, resort, campus or new city, will be organised and built in stages: its land, buildings, streets, open spaces and infrastructure.

What a master plan contains

A master plan usually includes a vision, a land-use plan, a road and movement network, public open space, density and building height, infrastructure, phasing, and design guidelines for the buildings and spaces that follow.

Where master planning is used

Master plans guide new cities and towns, coastal resorts, university campuses, mixed-use districts, tourism destinations and the regeneration of city centres. PUD's master plans include Marina El Alamein, Heliopark and New Najaf City.

From concept to delivery

A concept master plan tests the idea. A detailed master plan fixes the rules. Delivery then depends on design guidelines, infrastructure packages and a clear phasing order, which is where advisory support from the planners continues.

Related question

How long does a master plan take?

It depends on the size and the approvals needed. A concept master plan can be prepared in months; detailed master plans, guidelines and phasing for a new city take longer.
