How we work

We start by listening: stakeholder engagement and a close reading of the place. Then we assemble the team each project needs from our network of planners, architects, designers, sustainability experts, economists and social scientists. Six principles guide every project: insight-driven planning, scenario-based thinking, collaborative co-creation, legacy-oriented design, agile project structuring and integrated sustainability.
