Questions about PUD.

Frequently asked questions

Is PUD Consultants related to a planned unit development (PUD)?

No. PUD Consultants is an urban planning and architecture practice in Cairo. The acronym stands for Planning and Urban Development.

Who is PUD Consultants?

PUD Consultants (Planning & Urban Development Consultants) plans cities, regions and destinations, and designs the buildings and landscapes inside them. Prof. Dr. Abdallah Attia founded the practice in Cairo in 1966, and Prof. Dr. Amr Attia leads it today from offices in Cairo, Riyadh and Dubai.

What does PUD do?

PUD offers six services: urban and regional planning and city making; urban design and master planning; architectural and experiential design; environmentally sustainable development; cultural heritage and identity preservation; and strategic advisory and green economy. Tourism destinations, landscape and infrastructure are planned within them.

Where does PUD work?

PUD has worked in 11 countries: Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Iraq, Syria, the UAE, Qatar, Iran, Germany, Cameroon and The Gambia. Most of its projects are in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.

Who founded PUD and who leads it now?

Prof. Dr. Abdallah Abdel Aziz Attia founded PUD in 1966. His son, Prof. Dr. Amr Attia, joined in 1991, became CEO in 2005 and leads the practice today. He is also Professor of Urban Planning at Ain Shams University and an Eisenhower Fellow (2007).

How many projects has PUD done?

This site documents 192 PUD projects between 1964 and 2026 in 11 countries, each with its own page. They include built work, competition entries, master plans and advisory roles. The list grows as records are verified.

What is PUD's best-known work?

Marina El Alamein on Egypt's North Coast, Tut Amun on Lake Nasser, Hilton Dahab on the Gulf of Aqaba, New Najaf City, the Khaybar heritage area plan, the AlUla Strategic Framework Plan, the Pyramids Plateau destination plan, Jabal Thabeer park in Makkah and Sumou Boulevard in New Cairo.

Does PUD work with international firms?

Yes. PUD has collaborated with WATG on more than 30 projects and has worked with AECOM Arabia, Hill International, Gillespies, Systematica, HOK, Buro Happold, SHAKER Group, Dar Al-Handasah and others.

Who are PUD's clients?

Clients include national authorities such as the New Urban Communities Authority (NUCA), the Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs and Housing, the Royal Commission for AlUla and the Royal Commission for Makkah City and Holy Sites, as well as private developers and universities.

Does PUD plan sustainable cities?

Yes. PUD designed the Abo Dabbab marine field station on the Red Sea, worked on the Green Star Hotels initiative with GIZ and contributed as sustainability expert to WATG's Sharaan Nature Reserve master plan in AlUla.

Where are PUD's offices?

Cairo (Beverly Hills in Sheikh Zayed City, and Heliopolis), Riyadh (Diplomatic Quarter and Al Nakheel) and Dubai (Palm Jumeirah). Full addresses and phone numbers are on the contact page.

Does PUD work on heritage sites?

Yes. PUD is the cultural-heritage specialist on the WATG team for the Giza Pyramids Plateau destination plan and prepared the development brief for the Khaybar heritage area master plan.
