Landscape planning and design

Parks, waterfronts and open space are where a plan meets daily life. We design them to be used, easy to reach and at home in their landscape.

Parks, waterfronts, plazas and green corridors are where a plan meets daily life. PUD plans and designs landscapes at the scale of a hillside park, a resort garden or a regional green network.

In Makkah, PUD is the landscape design partner, with Gillespies, Studio 4215 and Systematica, for Jabal Thabeer, a hillside park of about 250,000 square metres at Mina. The team was first runner-up in Future Landscapes (Unbuilt) at the Landscape Middle East Awards 2025.

What this covers

  • Parks and public open space
  • Waterfront and resort landscape
  • Hillside and desert landscape
  • Green infrastructure
  • Movement and access in open space
  • Landscape guidelines
