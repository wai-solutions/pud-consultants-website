Environmentally sustainable development

We embed sustainability into every layer: low-carbon strategies, green infrastructure and ecological systems that future-proof projects and meet global standards.

Sustainability at PUD means environmental, social and cultural balance. It shows in projects such as the Abo Dabbab marine field station on the Red Sea, the Green Star Hotels initiative for sustainable hotels and destinations in Egypt with GIZ, and WATG's Sharaan Nature Reserve master plan in AlUla, to which PUD contributed as sustainability expert.

The practice works to the UN Sustainable Development Goals and follows six principles on every project: insight-driven planning, scenario-based thinking, collaborative co-creation, legacy-oriented design, agile project structuring and integrated sustainability.

  • Environmental impact and resilience
  • Green infrastructure and low-carbon planning
  • Green hotels and destinations
