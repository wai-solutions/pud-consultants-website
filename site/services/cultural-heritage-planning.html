Cultural heritage planning and urban conservation

Heritage is not a backdrop. We build it into the plan, so new development continues the story of a place instead of replacing it.

Heritage sites are living places. We read the archaeology, the landscape and the community together, then give each its place in the plan.

PUD works on heritage areas such as Khaybar and AlUla in Saudi Arabia, the Giza Pyramids Plateau destination as the cultural-heritage specialist on the WATG team, and urban renewal in Historic Cairo around the aqueducts of Magra El Oyoun.

What this covers

  • Heritage area master plans
  • Archaeology and Egyptology advisory
  • Urban conservation and renewal
  • Cultural interpretation and storytelling
  • Visitor and community access
  • Heritage-led tourism
