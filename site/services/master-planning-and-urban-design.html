Expertise

Urban design and master planning

We lead large-scale urban development, shaping mixed-use destinations and smart districts with strategic zoning, land optimisation and future-ready urban systems.

A master plan fixes how a place is organised: streets, public space, land uses, density, infrastructure, phasing and identity. Urban design gives it human scale, so the result is walkable, comfortable and easy to read.

PUD has prepared master plans since the 1970s. They include Marina El Alamein, a large-scale coastal resort on Egypt's North West Coast; new city and campus plans in Iraq, Syria and Egypt; and destination master plans in Saudi Arabia and Egypt with partners such as WATG and AECOM.

Tourism runs through PUD's history. Prof. Dr. Abdallah Attia planned Egypt's North West Coast and Marina El Alamein, and designed Tut Amun on Lake Nasser and Hilton Dahab in Sinai. His son, Prof. Dr. Amr Attia, studied tourism policy on that same coast for his PhD at University College London.

Parks, waterfronts, plazas and green corridors are where a plan meets daily life. PUD plans and designs landscapes at the scale of a hillside park, a resort garden or a regional green network.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between urban planning and master planning?

Urban planning works at city and regional scale and sets policy, structure and the direction of growth. Master planning works at the scale of a site, district or new community and shows how it will be laid out and phased. PUD does both, and a regional plan often leads straight into the master plan for a district inside it.

Which master plans is PUD known for?

Marina El Alamein on Egypt's North Coast, New Najaf City in Iraq, Heliopark in New Cairo, the Alamein Riviera vision, the Pyramids Plateau destination plan and the Khaybar heritage area plan are among the best known.

What is sustainable tourism planning?

Sustainable tourism planning shapes destinations so that the economic, social and environmental effects of visitors are managed over the long term. It looks at land use, infrastructure, local communities, heritage and ecology together.

What landscape projects has PUD worked on?

PUD is the landscape design partner for the Jabal Thabeer hillside park at Mina in Makkah, first runner-up at the Landscape Middle East Awards 2025, and advised on a park at Muzdalifah. It has also prepared landscape design for a resort in Riyadh.
