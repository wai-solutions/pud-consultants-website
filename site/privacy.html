Privacy

Privacy.

This page explains what happens to the details you send PUD Consultants through this website.

What we collect

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, a topic and your message. Nothing else is collected. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

How it is sent

The form passes your message to the PUD team by email, through FormSubmit, an email relay service. You can also write to info@pudconsultants.com directly.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on it. We do not sell them, share them for marketing or add you to a mailing list.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, write to info@pudconsultants.com.
