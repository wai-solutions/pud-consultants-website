Projects / Saudi Arabia

Urban planning and design in Makkah, Saudi Arabia.

PUD has 10 projects in Makkah, Saudi Arabia between 2017 and 2026. They include Jabal Thabeer Park (Mina, Makkah), Kidana Al Khaif (Mina, Muzdalifah and Arafat) and Jorhum Destination. The work spans advisory, competition, infrastructure, landscape, masterplan, mixed use and regional planning.
