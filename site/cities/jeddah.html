Projects / Saudi Arabia

Urban planning and design in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

PUD has 11 projects in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia between 2011 and 2020. They include Al Hamdaniyah Investment Lands Concept Master Plan, Jeddah, Al Jawhara Residence and Jeddah Spatial Framework Plan. The work spans advisory, competition, housing, infrastructure, masterplan, mixed use, regional planning and tourism.
