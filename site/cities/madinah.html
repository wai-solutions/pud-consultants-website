Projects / Saudi Arabia

Urban planning and design in Madinah, Saudi Arabia.

PUD has seven projects in Madinah, Saudi Arabia between 1994 and 2020, including Al Hashimi, Al Kattan, Al Shirbini and Al Zahem Hotels and Residential Complexes, Al Ras Al Abiyad Pre-Concept Master Plan and Al Kattan Hotel, Commercial and Residential Complex (Concept Design). The work covers mixed-use developments, hotels and master plans.
