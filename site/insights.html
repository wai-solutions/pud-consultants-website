Urban planning, explained.

Short guides from a practice that has planned cities, coasts and holy sites since 1966, with the numbers behind the work.

Guide · 2 min readWhat is urban planning?Urban planning decides how land in cities and regions is used, connected and looked after, so that people can live, work and move well.→Guide · 2 min readWhat is master planning?A master plan shows how one defined area, a district, resort, campus or new city, will be organised and built in stages: its land, buildings, streets, open spaces and infrastructure.→Guide · 2 min readWhat is sustainable tourism planning?Sustainable tourism planning keeps visitor numbers, buildings and infrastructure in balance with local communities, culture and the environment, so a destination stays worth visiting.→Egypt · 2 min readUrban planning in Egypt: from the Nile Valley to the new coast and capitalMost of Egypt's people live on a small share of its land, along the Nile and Delta, so national planning has long pushed growth towards new communities, coasts and desert corridors.→Saudi Arabia · 2 min readUrban planning in Saudi Arabia: giga-projects, holy sites and regional plansSaudi Arabia is planning at every scale at once: national land strategies, giga-project destinations, holy-site programmes and the growth of Riyadh, Jeddah and Makkah.→DestinationPlanning Red Sea destinationsPort Ghalib, Abo Dabbab, Hurghada, Ain El Sokhna and the Yanbu to Duba sub-regional plan.→
